## Cross-run registry and strategic registration lock

**Benchmark/lock lifecycle (normative, single ordering):** Freeze the reviewed seed-plan hash, schedules/rosters, collision scans, guards and required rejection tests first. Before execution, preflight and append every intended workload registration plus validation/final reservation metadata; every benchmark attempt references its exact earlier registration. Only registered benchmark attempts may execute pre-lock. Append each attempt record only after the exact remote version is terminal; unresolved/nonterminal attempts cannot be recorded as complete or source evidence. Once eligible benchmark attempts are terminal and a successful result is complete, append its immutable `benchmark_artifact` with content hash, current pre-lock head/index, and exact registration and terminal-attempt event/hash anchors. The next event MUST be `registration_closed`, at the next index with no event interposed; pin its resulting hash as `lock_head_hash`. No benchmark or other workload runs after lock; training/imitation/strategic runs registered pre-lock may start only post-lock. The artifact cannot pin a future lock hash. After lock, append a separate `benchmark_attestation` for each artifact, binding its unchanged content hash, original prefix, registration/terminal-attempt anchors and lock hash. Never rewrite the artifact; downstream use requires a valid attestation. Reject pre-registration benchmark attempts, nonterminal attempt records, artifact without terminal successful attempt, any event between artifact and lock, benchmark after lock, and missing/stale/mismatched attestation. Benchmark evidence remains limited to runtime, transition/action parity, throughput/timing; no strategic outputs are permitted.

One append-only global registry is authoritative. Events use consecutive zero-based indexes, genesis `prev_event_hash` is 64 zeroes, and canonical UTF-8 JSON payload has exactly `event_index`, `event_type`, `event_data`, `prev_event_hash` (NFC strings, sorted keys, compact separators, no floats); `event_hash` is SHA-256 of payload bytes and is excluded from its own payload. Before locking, pin the prefix head and final index. The next `registration_closed` event uses next index and prefix head as `prev_event_hash`, carries `pre_lock_head_hash` and `pre_lock_final_event_index` in its data, and is hashed normally; pin its resulting hash as `lock_head_hash` on post-lock workload/evidence outputs (pre-lock benchmark evidence follows the separate attestation lifecycle below). This eliminates self-reference. The chain is tamper-evident against partial/inconsistent edits, reorder, deletion or mutation only while independently captured head anchors remain; it is not a signature and cannot detect full rewrite of registry and all anchors. Do not add signing or paid services. Every workload registration binds unique UUID, type/ablation, code/config/opponent hashes, finite bounds, roots, shared spec digest, full expanded roster/digest and scan, preflight estimate/allocation and caps.

All intended workload registrations and validation/final reservation metadata are appended and budget-checked before lock. Only a registered benchmark attempt may execute before lock, and its attempt event is appended only after terminal status for its exact remote version. Append the immutable artifact only after a terminal successful attempt; append `registration_closed` immediately next with no intervening registry event. Benchmark and all other workload attempts are forbidden after lock; registered train/imitation/ablation runs execute only post-lock. Validation/final remain reservation-only pre-lock, validation may execute post-lock only for promotion of registered candidates on frozen validation, and final remains final-evaluator-only after candidate freeze. Benchmark PPO returns/rewards remain invocation-only and discarded; no strategic output enters benchmark evidence. The immutable pre-lock artifact binds its creation-time prefix/index and exact registration/terminal-attempt events; a separate post-lock attestation binds that artifact and original anchors to the lock. Tests cover ordering, registration/terminal gates, artifact-to-lock adjacency, benchmark post-lock rejection, training pre-lock rejection, hash self-reference, tamper/attestation cases, evaluation access rules and the limited threat model.
